DragonFruit is the recommended slicer for Athena printers.

Get DragonFruit

DragonFruit runs on your computer. Download and install it to get started.

Download DragonFruit

Windows: An unsigned application warning may appear. Select More info and allow the installer to run.

Connect Your Athena

  1. Launch DragonFruit. Select Concepts3D, then choose your Athena printer model.
  2. Select the correct LCD resolution, shown in the WebUI navbar.
  3. Make sure your printer is powered on and connected to the same local network as your computer.
  4. Use Auto Detect to find the printer, or enter its IP address manually.

Slice & Send to Athena

  1. Prepare your model, orientation, supports, hollowing, drain holes and raft as needed.
  2. Select Export.
  3. Confirm the correct Athena printer and Material / resin profile. Configure Anti-Aliasing if supported.
  4. Select Slice & Upload to slice the job and send it directly to the printer.
Recommended: Slice & Upload

For normal printing, use Slice & Upload. The uploaded job will appear on the WebUI Jobs page and printer touchscreen.

Slice to File saves a .nanodlp file for manual or USB transfer. Slice & Print uploads the job and immediately starts the printer, so use it only when the printer is fully prepared to print.
