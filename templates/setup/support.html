Diagnostic data & privacy

By submitting this form, you agree that your printer configuration and log files will be transmitted to a server operated by Concepts3D or its affiliates.

Connectivity

Cloud Connectivity is required to use your printer in the Athena Mobile App. When enabled, printer data is transmitted to servers operated by Concepts3D in Europe or by partners globally.

Cloud data & privacy

By enabling Cloud Connectivity, you agree that anonymous usage statistics are collected and processed by Concepts3D and its affiliates. This includes completed prints, number of layers printed and print duration, among other information.

To make printer data available in the app, the following information is transmitted:

  • Machine identifier and serial number
  • Machine status, including resin profiles and usage statistics
  • Camera feed, if available
  • Print jobs available on the machine (without preview images or STL files)
  • Print progress and associated status messages

The separate print-asset sync option enables sharing job previews and timelapses for access in the app.
